# Ethics & Responsible Use

WiFi sensing can detect people **through walls and without their knowledge**. That makes it
powerful for elder care, fall detection and smart homes, and also dangerous for surveillance.
This project studies the technology openly so that its risks are understood and defenses can be
built.

## Principles

1. **Consent first.** Only collect CSI or video of people who have given informed consent.
   Never capture data of neighbors, roommates, the public or anyone unaware.
2. **No covert surveillance.** Do not contribute code, guides or tools whose primary purpose is
   to secretly monitor people. Such PRs will be closed.
3. **Protect data.** Do not commit raw recordings, faces, or personally identifying data.
   Anonymize and document how any shared data was collected.
4. **Follow the law and your institution.** Get ethics/IRB approval where required for human
   subject research.
5. **Balance with defense.** We actively welcome research on detecting and preventing unwanted
   WiFi sensing.
6. **Be honest about limits.** Report failure cases (e.g. unseen rooms) as clearly as successes.

## Reporting concerns
If you see content in this repo that violates these principles, open an issue or contact the
maintainers privately via GitHub.
